This snapshot reflects where you stand today as you look to grow RISE Modern Wellness beyond your Vero Beach location. It's not a rejection and it's not a final verdict — it's a clear, honest picture of what you bring to the table and where the next steps naturally fall.

Your Readiness Profile
Operator Readiness & Capability

You're already running a mid-size wellness operation successfully, with years of experience across wholesale, real estate, and service businesses behind you. This isn't theoretical — you've done the work of building and operating a team, and it shows.

Vision, Motivation & Commitment

You know exactly what you want: a specific number of locations, a role helping other owners succeed, and a clear picture of what it means for your family and community. That clarity is rare and it's going to serve you well — the only thing to sharpen is pacing the plan to match what the capital and build-out timelines can realistically support.

Sector & Multi-Unit Fit

Health, Wellness & Fitness is exactly where you already are, and you've proven the model works at the unit level. Moving to multiple units is a natural next step — the main shift will be building out the team and management layer that lets you run several locations without doing it all yourself.

Coachability & Ecosystem Fit

You're results-driven and bring a genuinely innovative streak to how you think about the business. As you scale, it's worth staying close to the RISE playbook where it's already working — the brand's consistency is part of what makes multi-unit growth possible, even as you bring your own ideas to the table.

Strengths, Growth Areas & Opportunities

What You Bring

  • A proven, successfully operating RISE location as your foundation
  • Strong financial standing — net worth and credit that comfortably clear typical financing thresholds
  • A multi-industry background (wholesale, real estate, service) that translates directly into running and growing a business
  • A capital plan already in motion — financing, an investor, and real estate equity all part of the picture

Where to Grow

  • Building out a management team so you're not personally running every location
  • Mapping your available cash alongside your full financing plan so the numbers line up unit by unit
  • Pacing your timeline to match realistic build-out schedules for new locations

What FN Can Unlock

  • Help structuring a multi-unit development conversation with RISE corporate
  • Support sequencing your capital so each new location is fully funded before it opens
  • Territory guidance so new locations complement rather than compete with existing RISE units nearby
  • A direct answer on international development options, so you know where that fits in your timeline

Things to Stay Ahead Of

  • International expansion is an exciting idea, but it's not yet a confirmed path with RISE — worth a direct conversation before it becomes part of the plan
  • Opening several locations quickly can outpace your ability to staff and manage them well — building your team early helps you grow without losing quality
  • Keep the plan flexible on timing; the right pace beats the fastest pace
Your Recommended Capital Pathway
SBA Financing + Outside Investor + Real Estate Equity
Fund Unit Two First, Then Scale From There
Additional option: Phased single-unit financing as each location proves out

Your outlined capital — SBA financing, an outside investor, and your real estate equity — comfortably covers a second RISE location. Getting to 3–5 units is absolutely within reach; the smart path is opening and stabilizing that second unit first, then using its performance and additional financing to fund the next ones, rather than trying to raise everything at once.

Your path forward
  1. Discovery call: map out which unit comes first, what it costs, and how your current capital covers it
  2. Corporate conversation: talk to RISE directly about multi-unit development terms and territory options near you
  3. Team building: start identifying who could step into a management role as you add locations
  4. Territory selection: narrow down 2–3 East Coast metros for your next location, outside areas RISE already serves
