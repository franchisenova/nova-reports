⚑ Formula issues found in the working Excel file: (1) Corporate-store labor is fixed at one store’s $349,920 no matter how many stores are open; every other cost is multiplied by store count. (2) The moderate and aggressive cases sell at higher AUVs but use the conservative case’s per-store costs, per-store royalty and $25K fee, so they’re overstated further; some of their system-sales figures are typed in rather than calculated. (3) Brand-fund contributions are counted as franchisor revenue. (4) “Free cash flow” is half of EBITDA, with no deduction for the cost of building new corporate stores (≈$2.1M for six in the conservative case at $350K each), taxes or debt service. New stores also earn a full year in their opening year. (5) The valuation’s “6x Year 3 EBITDA” actually multiplies Year 3 free cash flow. Fixing only the labor formula, their own valuation method drops from a $10.35M low-end average to about $6.7M.

  • Minimal debt: one $112K truck loan covered 12.9x

⚑ Red Flags / Watch Items

  • Deck’s “$441K net income” is operating income; actual net income is $327K. The one-pager’s own “27%” doesn’t match its $465K ÷ $1.37M
