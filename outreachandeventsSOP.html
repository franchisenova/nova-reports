1. Definitions

Everyone and everything BG comes into contact with falls into one of these buckets. Get the event coordinator directions in Section 2 right, and these numbers take care of themselves.

BG
Initiative		 Which of BG's seven initiatives a lead belongs to — Franchise Nova, BG Enterprise, Wealth Through Women's Wellbeing, Refuge, Preservation Home & Land, Equipped, or Proximate Leaders. Set on every Contact the moment they come in.
Direct Participants
(Count)		 A team member is speaking, presenting, or moderating — a webinar, panel, or talk — where we know a specific number of people were reached, but did not capture individual names or contact info. Logged as one number on the event.
Direct Participants
(Confirmed)		 We captured a name + email/phone — at our booth or table, a registration, a QR code scan, or a business card entered into HubSpot. This is not a typed-in number — it's the live count of Contacts associated with this event's deal, driven by the Attended association label. See Section 2 for how that gets applied.
Indirect
Participants		 People present at an event we had a presence at, but who we can't confirm any direct engagement with — the rest of a conference floor, a room's total headcount beyond who we actually spoke with. Logged as one number on the event, separate from Direct Count.
Capital
Consortium		 Banks and capital partners in our referral network for candidate capital access. Tracked at the Company level by status — not counted alongside participant metrics. See Section 5 for the full status list.
Not tracked at all: we had no formal presence at an event — attending as a guest, wandering a conference floor with no booth or speaking role. No aggregate number gets logged. Anyone individually engaged in that setting still goes in as Direct.
Registered ≠ attended: a registration alone doesn't make someone a Direct Participant. Only people carrying the Attended association label on that event's deal count toward Direct Participants (Confirmed) — registrants who never show up keep only the Registered label, which automatically excludes them from the count. Nobody manually removes them from anything.

2. Event coordinators — adding & closing out events

Every event or activity gets logged as a deal in the Outreach & Events Log pipeline — but not every event happens the same way, so the steps depend on which of these three describes it.

PathWhen this appliesWhat happens
1. We're hosting Our own event — our booth, our table, our registration. Registration and check-in forms plus their workflows apply the Registered and Attended labels. Direct Participants (Confirmed) populates live.
2. Someone else hosts, we get a list We didn't run registration, but received names/emails afterward. Bulk import the list as Contacts, associating them to the Deal at import time. No forms or workflows needed.
3. Someone else hosts, count only We only know a total number — no names. Type the number directly into Direct Participants (Count) or Indirect Participants on the Deal. No Contacts involved at all.
Why this works for repeat attendees: attendance is recorded as a labeled association between a Contact and that event's specific Deal — not as a status on the Contact. Someone who attends three events gets three separate labeled associations, so nothing overwrites and every event counts them correctly.

Path 1 — We're hosting

Two forms drive this path: a registration form (before) and a check-in form (at the door). Each writes a shared Event Code that matches the Contact to the right event Deal, and workflows apply the Registered and Attended association labels automatically.

Event Code naming: INITIATIVE-EVENTNAME-YEAR, all caps, hyphens only. Prefixes: FN (Franchise Nova), BGE (BG Enterprise), WWW (Wealth Through Women's Wellbeing), REF (Refuge), PHL (Preservation Home & Land), EQP (Equipped), PL (Proximate Leaders). Examples: FN-INVESTFEST-2026, REF-GALA-2026, BGE-SUMMIT-2026. If the same event runs twice in a year, add the month: FN-INVESTFEST-MAR-2026.

Before the event — creating it

1
Create the deal. Pipeline: Outreach & Events Log. Stage: Planned. Deal Name: the event name and date.
Event coordinator
2
Set Event Code, Engagement Type, Event Date, and Partnered Event / Partner Name on the deal. The Event Code is what ties every registrant and attendee to this specific event — it must be unique and exactly match what goes on the forms.
HubSpot property
3
Clone the registration form template. Update the intro copy and thank-you message to reference this event, and set the hidden Event Code field to this event's code.
Event coordinator
4
Clone the check-in form template. Set its hidden Event Code field to the same code. This is the form used at the door — keep it to just email so check-in is fast.
Event coordinator
5
Clone both workflow templates — the Registration workflow and the Attendance workflow. On each, point the trigger at the matching form you just cloned, and set the BG Initiative value for this event. Turn both on.
Automatic (once built)
6
Leave Direct Participants (Count) and Indirect Participants blank for now — these get filled in by hand after the event, and only if applicable. Direct Participants (Confirmed) needs no manual field at all.

At the event — check-in and walk-ups

Have the check-in form open on a tablet or phone at the door, or share it as a QR code. Anyone who checks in through it gets the Attended label automatically, whether or not they registered in advance — so walk-ups are handled by the same form as everyone else, with no separate process.

If you used a paper sign-in sheet instead

If check-in happened on paper rather than through the form, mark everyone as attended in one bulk pass afterward:

1
Go to Contacts and click Advanced filters → + Filter → Event Code → select this event's code. That gives you everyone who registered.
Event coordinator
2
Select everyone who actually attended, per the sign-in sheet — leave no-shows unchecked.
3
Click the More dropdown → Enroll in workflow → "Apply Attended Label." That applies the Attended label to everyone selected in one pass.
Applies label automatically
Do this within a few days of the event. This bulk method matches on the contact's current Event Code — if someone registers for a different event before you close this one out, their code gets overwritten and they won't appear in the filter. Their existing associations are safe either way; it's only this filter that depends on the current value.

After the event — closing it out

The event coordinator is responsible for closing out the deal within a few business days.

1
If applicable, fill in Direct Participants (Count). The number of people reached when a team member spoke, presented, or moderated at this event, who did not hand over contact info.
2
If applicable, fill in Indirect Participants. The total known audience/attendance size at the event, minus anyone already counted as Direct — only if we had an active presence and a total is knowable.
3
Double-check Partnered Event / Partner Name is accurate — sometimes this isn't confirmed until the day of the event.
4
Move the deal stage to Completed.
HubSpot property
5
Add a note to the deal with anything worth remembering — attendance quality, follow-up promises made, standout candidates.
HubSpot note
6
Nothing to do for Direct Participants (Confirmed). Open the deal's associated Contacts to see exactly who's labeled Attended, or check the Investor Dashboard for the rolled-up number — both pull from the same live associations, so they always match.

Path 2 — Someone else hosts, we get a list

No form of ours was involved, so there's nothing to register through — this path skips forms and workflows entirely.

1
Create the deal as usual. Set Event Code, Engagement Type, Event Date, and Partnered Event = Yes with Partner Name set to whoever actually hosted it.
Event coordinator
2
Bulk-import the list as Contacts using HubSpot's Import tool. During the same import, set BG Initiative and this event's Event Code for every row, and associate each imported Contact to this event's Deal with the Attended label.
Event coordinator
3
If the import can't apply the label directly, set the Event Code during import, then filter Contacts by that Event Code and bulk-enroll them in the "Apply Attended Label" workflow — the same method used for paper sign-in sheets in Path 1.
4
Move the deal stage to Completed once the import is done.
HubSpot property

Path 3 — Someone else hosts, count only

No names, no import, no Contacts at all — just a number.

1
Create the deal as usual. Set Engagement Type, Event Date, and Partnered Event / Partner Name.
Event coordinator
2
Type the number directly onto the deal. Direct Participants (Count) if this was a specific direct-engagement moment (a team member spoke or moderated) with a known reach but no names; Indirect Participants if it's just a general attendance total with no specific engagement.
3
Move the deal stage to Completed. Nothing else to set up — no forms, no workflows, no Contacts.
HubSpot property

3. Leads vs. the Pipeline

StageDefinition
LeadA Direct or Indirect connection we're now nurturing — has not yet completed an intake form.
In the PipelineHas completed an intake form (or, for certain partner-side records — see below — been manually entered). This is the only trigger that moves someone from Lead to Pipeline.
Partner-side records (organizations or systems we work with directly, rather than individual candidates): the intake form isn't built for these. If real work is happening with one of these without a formal intake/agreement step, manually add them to the pipeline at whatever stage reflects the actual work underway.
One-off / informal engagements (e.g. connecting a candidate directly to a capital partner without the full program): going forward, everyone should go through an intake form unless it's a genuine one-off. One-offs still get placed into the pipeline at whatever stage they're actually at — Contacts and Deals both live in the one unified pipeline view (see the Dashboard & Pipeline Build Guide).
Re-engaging with a different BG initiative mid-nurture: if a contact is still at Lead when they engage with a different initiative, their current nurture pauses and they're flagged for a human decision. If they're already further along, the new initiative just gets added to their record and their existing progress continues untouched. Mechanics live in the BG Initiative Tagging & Cross-Nurture Coordination doc.

4. When a Lead becomes a Deal

A candidate becomes a Deal in HubSpot when they sign the participation agreement. The signed agreement is what creates the Deal record, and each contact gets added to a deal pipeline depending on the BG Initiative.
