What This Actually Tells Us

This reads differently than a pre-FDD candidate. Wrinkle Free has done real groundwork — a drafted FDD, an operations manual (the Brand Standards Manual), a decade of operating history, and three signed franchise agreements already in the pipeline. That's meaningfully further along than a system with nothing on paper yet.

But where the framework can actually be scored against real disclosures, four of five dimensions come back Red, and they're not soft judgment calls — they're grounded in the FDD's own language: no financial performance representations, zero franchised outlets ever opened, no franchisor financing of any kind, and exit terms structured to limit what a franchisee's business is worth at sale. Layered on top of a three-year revenue decline and negative equity at the operating level, this is a different kind of conversation than Jollof Bowl's "not yet assessable" — this is assessable, and several of what it shows are real flags rather than open questions.

What's Working in Wrinkle Free's Favor

  • Ten years of real operating history (since 2015) — far longer than a typical pre-franchise concept.
  • A drafted FDD, Franchise Agreement, and Brand Standards Manual already exist — real infrastructure most pre-FDD candidates lack.
  • Three franchise agreements already signed — genuine market interest, not just projections.
  • Leadership includes real relevant experience — a decade running the business, plus a COO with an enterprise consulting background.
  • A defined, capital-efficient unit model (pickup-and-delivery, lower real estate cost than a traditional laundromat).

07Open Questions for the Meeting

Ask Wrinkle Free Directly

  • What's driving three consecutive years of revenue decline and the 5.7-point gross margin compression — is this a temporary or structural trend?
  • Why have distributions exceeded net income in two of the last three years, and what's the plan for the negative equity position?
  • What's the current status of the word-mark trademark reconsideration, and was the Statement of Use filed on the design mark before its deadline?
  • What is the current status of the 3 signed-but-unopened franchise agreements — timelines, any that have lapsed?
  • How does the 45–55% gross margin / 20–30% EBITDA margin in the growth projections reconcile with the flagship unit's actual 2025 performance?
  • Why does the FDD provide no Item 19 financial performance representations — is that a deliberate choice, or is one in development?
  • Can they resolve the $25,000 vs. $1,500 transfer-fee discrepancy between the FDD and the Franchise Agreement?
  • Which of the marquee enterprise accounts listed are currently active, and what share of revenue do they represent?

Decide Internally as a Team

  • Given four of five System Nova Score dimensions read Red on real FDD disclosures rather than "unscored" data gaps, does this change the bar for what BLK GRVTY needs to see resolved before any partnership discussion moves forward?
  • How much weight to give the ten-year operating history and existing FDD infrastructure against the financial trend and franchisor-support risks?
  • What would BLK GRVTY need from Wrinkle Free — audited financials, a rebuilt Item 19, or franchisor capitalization — before a partnership could be recommended?

08Discussion Framing

Wrinkle Free is further along on paper than a pre-FDD candidate — a decade of operating history, a drafted FDD and operations manual, and three signed franchise agreements already in the pipeline. That's real groundwork most early-stage Zor candidates don't have.

But where BLK GRVTY's own System Nova Score framework can be applied against actual disclosures rather than gaps, the read is largely unfavorable: three straight years of revenue decline, margin compression, negative equity, no Item 19 disclosure, no franchisor financing, and exit terms that limit what a franchisee's business is worth at sale — several of these sourced directly from the FDD's own language, including its own highlighted risk about the franchisor's financial condition.

Recommended framing for the meeting: treat this less as an open evaluation and more as a request for specific answers on a specific, sourced set of concerns. The Document Checklist above is the agenda — what Wrinkle Free can resolve in the room (trademark status, the margin discrepancy, the pipeline status) versus what would need real follow-up (audited financials, franchisor capitalization) before this moves further.
