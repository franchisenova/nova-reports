Molly Maid — Proceed to Discovery Day · Jan-Pro as Second Conversation

Molly Maid is the right first move for Jordan. Jan-Pro Regional is the second conversation — specifically the regional developer path, not unit level. The regional developer structure is the most naturally semi-absentee model on this analysis and creates a passive income layer (royalty income from unit franchisees) that Molly Maid and Meineke do not. Meineke is not recommended at this time. It's worth revisiting if Jordan's real estate equity turns out to be substantial and if he has a strong automotive background to manage the licensed service manager requirement, but it doesn't fit the current capital picture.
