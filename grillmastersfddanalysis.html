Item 5 — Initial Fee Green
$25,000 single unit PRIMARY
Fully non-refundable once paid, "deemed fully earned upon payment." No stated multi-unit fee discount — franchisor retains sole discretion to reduce, defer, or waive the fee case-by-case, which is not a guaranteed structure a candidate can rely on. Under the $45K threshold, so Green on fee size alone, but the waiver language is soft — nothing is contractually locked in.
Item 6 — Fee Burden Green
~10.5% minimum — Royalty 7.5% (or $1,000/mo floor) + Ad Fund 2% + Local 1% PRIMARY
The $1,000/month royalty floor means the effective rate is higher than 7.5% at low Gross Sales — e.g., at $10K/month in sales, the floor pushes the royalty to 10% instead of 7.5%. The Advertising Fund has no operating history (new franchisor, no prior contributions or spend to evaluate). Local marketing spend (1% of Gross Sales) is a separate, uncapped obligation.
Item 7 — Investment Range Amber
$46,200–$131,400 · Planning amt: $144,540 PRIMARY
Wide range (nearly 3x low-to-high) driven mainly by build-out ($2K–$35K) and working capital ($10K–$30K) variability. Low end is genuinely accessible for a retail concept. All figures are franchisor estimates with no operating precedent — the franchisor has only ever built out its own single company location, so these ranges haven't been stress-tested by an actual franchisee.
Item 10 — Franchisor Financing Red
No franchisor financing · No guarantee assistance · SBA status not disclosed PRIMARY
FDD states plainly: "We do not offer direct or indirect financing... nor do we provide assistance in securing financing from third parties." No preferred lender relationships, no SBA registration status confirmed anywhere in the document. As a first-time franchisor with zero franchised units, it's unlikely this system is yet listed on the SBA Franchise Directory — that needs direct confirmation before any candidate builds a capital stack assuming SBA eligibility.
Item 11 — Ownership Model Amber
Owner (or managing owner) must personally operate · manager permitted for day-to-day PRIMARY
Item 15: "You (or your managing owner) are obligated to participate personally in the direct operation of the Business," though a trained manager may handle day-to-day tasks. This is not semi-absentee by design — it's owner-operator with a staffing allowance, not the passive-ownership structure some FN candidates specifically seek.
Item 12 — Territory Red
No exclusive territory — franchisor reserves right to place units nearby PRIMARY
"You will not receive an exclusive territory... we do not grant exclusive rights to any geographic area." Franchisor explicitly reserves the right to license other locations "regardless of proximity," operate company units nearby, and sell through other channels (e-commerce, pop-ups, other brands). This is one of the weakest territory protections in FN's system library — a real risk to any single-unit candidate's long-term market position.
Item 19 — Financial Performance Red
1 company outlet only · Gross Sales $218,839 → $403,666 (+84.5% YoY) PRIMARY
Disclosed figures represent 100% of "the system" — but the system is a single company-owned outlet. Zero franchised outlets have ever operated, so there is no franchisee-side data of any kind. The 84.5% YoY growth is real but drawn from one location's books, unaudited, over a two-year window — not statistically meaningful for underwriting a franchisee's expected performance. Supplementary figures — COGS $87,535 → $161,466 and Gross Profit $131,303 → $242,199 (a consistent ~60% gross margin both years) — come from a separate internal analysis sheet SECONDARY, not from the FDD text itself, and should be treated as unverified until confirmed against franchisor books directly.
Item 20 — System Health Red
0 franchised outlets ever sold or opened · 1 company outlet, flat PRIMARY
Tables 1–4 show zero franchise agreements signed, zero openings, zero terminations, zero transfers across 2023–2025 — because the system has never franchised. This is a pre-launch franchise offering in practical terms. Table 5 projects 3 new franchised outlets in GA next fiscal year, but this is a stated goal, not a track record. Churn rate is not calculable; there's simply no franchisee history to evaluate.
Item 21 — Franchisor Health Red
Unaudited balance sheet — every line item shows $0 PRIMARY
Exhibit A's balance sheet as of 12/21/2025 lists $0 across cash, receivables, inventory, PP&E, total assets, total liabilities, and total equity. This provides no verifiable information about the franchisor's financial capacity to support a franchise system — whether this reflects a genuine shell entity, an incomplete filing, or a template that wasn't populated, it needs direct clarification from the franchisor before this item can be scored as anything but a transparency gap.
Item 23 — Exit Rights Amber
Transfer fee $7,500 (30% of initial fee) · ROFR + franchisor buyback option PRIMARY
10-year term with one 10-year renewal, but renewal requires signing the "then-current franchise agreement," which may contain materially different terms. Franchisor holds a right of first refusal on any sale and a separate option to purchase the business at fair market value after termination or expiration. Combined with no exclusive territory (Item 12), the resale value of a single unit here is harder to establish than in a mature, territory-protected system.

FN System Assessment
The Grill Masters ATL — Caution / Not Yet Ready for Candidate Placement

This is, in practical terms, a pre-franchise-stage offering. The franchisor operates one company-owned outlet and has never sold or opened a franchised unit — every figure in Item 19 and Item 20 traces back to that single location. The revenue growth is genuinely strong (Gross Sales nearly doubled year-over-year, and the supplementary margin data suggests a healthy ~60% gross margin), but strong single-location economics at a founder-operated store don't tell FN anything about how a first-time franchisee, with a $1,000/mo royalty floor and no exclusive territory, would actually perform.

Two items stand out as structural, not cosmetic: Item 21's all-zero balance sheet leaves the franchisor's financial capacity to support a system completely unverified, and Item 12's lack of any exclusive territory is a real long-term risk to unit economics and resale value. Neither is disqualifying on its own — new franchisors often start exactly here — but both need direct answers from the franchisor before FN attaches a real candidate's capital to this system.

Recommend holding this system in the FN library as "Emerging / Monitor" rather than actively presenting it to candidates today. Revisit once the franchisor has (a) sold and opened its first 2–3 franchised units, (b) clarified Item 21's balance sheet, and (c) clarified SBA/financing pathway status.

What FN Can Unlock Here
  • Direct franchisor outreach to substantiate the Item 19 supplementary margin figures and request a corrected Exhibit A balance sheet
  • A structured re-review once the first franchised units open, to convert this from a single-outlet data point into an actual system-level Nova Score
  • If FN still wants to place a candidate here pre-launch, a bespoke risk disclosure and capital structure that assumes no SBA/franchisor financing support at all
Risks to Watch
  • Zero franchisee track record. Every disclosed figure comes from one company-owned outlet. There is no independent evidence this concept performs the same way under franchisee ownership, with franchisee-level royalty and ad-fund burden applied.
  • Franchisor financial health is unverified, not just weak. An all-zero balance sheet in Item 21 isn't a red flag about franchisor solvency — it's a red flag that the document provides no usable information either way. Get this clarified before any capital conversation.
  • No exclusive territory. The franchisor retains the right to place additional units nearby and sell through other channels. This affects both ongoing unit economics and long-term resale value.
  • No financing support and unconfirmed SBA status. Combined with a first-time franchisor's limited lending track record, a candidate should assume they need full capital coverage without franchisor-assisted financing.
  • Supplementary financial data is secondary-sourced. The COGS/gross-margin breakdown does not appear anywhere in the FDD text — it should be confirmed directly with the franchisor before it's presented to any candidate as verified.
