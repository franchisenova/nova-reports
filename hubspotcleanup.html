Team Brief · HubSpot Contact Cleanup

We're getting our data honest.

What's changed, what I need from you, and the new way we add and track every contact — so nobody slips through the cracks.

Our HubSpot has been telling us half-truths. Contacts with no names, no owners, no status, duplicates, dead emails — which means when we ask "where are we with this person," too often the honest answer is "we don't know."

That stops now. I've started a full cleanup and put new standards in place so that going forward, every contact has a real name, a real owner, and a real status. This brief covers what I've already done, the pieces I need your help on, and the new process for adding leads. It won't all happen at once — we'll take it in bite-size chunks — but it starts with everyone on the same page.

Why this is worth it: once our data is clean, our weekly pipeline review finally means something. We'll see true data on where every relationship stands and who's doing what — so no lead goes cold because nobody knew it was theirs.
01

What I've already done

  • Cleaned up duplicates. Merged duplicate contacts — we went from 2,546 down to 2,020 real records.
  • Recovered names. Found real names for 253 contacts we only had an email for.
  • Flagged dead emails. 49 records hard-bounced (invalid email) and are now marked as BG Lead Status Invalid Contact Info. I also built a workflow so any future invalid-email bounce is flagged automatically.
  • Built a real BG Lead Status system. One clean field that tells us where every contact stands — so "blank" finally means "needs attention," not "who knows."
  • Rebuilt Relationship Type. A cleaner set of values that reflects the true relationship we have with each person — which makes filtering for events and outreach actually reliable.
Heads up: BG Lead Status and Relationship Type both need to be set correctly for all our contacts, which is a big lift. We'll chunk it — the tasks below are the first bites.
02

The two fields that matter most

Learn these two and you understand the whole system. BG Lead Status = where they stand with us. Relationship Type = who they are to us. Every contact gets both.

Why we're retiring some older values: a few old options described what someone does in the world rather than their relationship to us — which made them impossible to filter reliably. Retiring them (and replacing with the real relationship) is what several tasks below are about:
  • Founder — describes their job, not their tie to us. A founder is really our Investor, Partner, Vendor, etc. — that's the tag we actually need. A founder could also be a Franchise Nova candidate looking to add another location or system — in that case tag them Candidate/Entrepreneur and set FN Contact Type to Zor.
  • BG Community — so broad it was true of almost everyone, so it told us nothing. It became the "I wasn't sure" tag.
  • Ecosystem builder — a field/sector label, not a relationship. Most are really Partners, Investors, or Service Providers.
  • GP / LP — fund roles, not a tie to BLK GRVTY. They're really Capital Partners or Investors.
  • Speaking Engagement — that's an activity (they spoke for us), not who they are. They're a Partner; speaking is tracked separately.
The impact: once these are replaced with true relationship types, filtering for an event or outreach actually returns the right people — instead of a mixed bag we can't trust. "Researcher" and "Investment Committee" are still under review.

What I need from you

Each of these is a HubSpot list. Open it, work through the contacts, and make the call described.

While you're in each record, set the core fields too. You're already in there — so let's get these contacts in the right place in the same pass:
  • Relationship Type — who they are
  • BG Lead Status — where they stand
  • BG Initiative — which program(s) they belong to
  • FN Contact Type (Zee / Zor) — only if they're a Franchise Nova candidate
  • Contact Owner — if it's blank and you know who it should be
The rule: set what you know, don't go researching. If you'd have to dig to figure something out, leave BG Lead Status as New – Unworked and move on — that flags it for triage later so nothing gets silently skipped.
