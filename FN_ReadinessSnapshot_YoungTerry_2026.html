Your Recommended Capital Pathway
SBA Lending · One Location at a Time
Fund Unit 1 · Prove It · Scale to Three
Additional option: Buy and convert an existing shop, supported by community lenders and local incentives

Your credit and financial history put you in a strong position to finance your first location with an SBA loan. Funding three new locations at once would ask a lot of your cash on hand, so the smartest path to all three is sequencing: open the strongest site first, let it build a track record, and use that momentum to finance the next two. Converting an existing shop can lower your cost to open and put customers in the door from day one.

Your path forward
  1. Confirm your AutoLab details: agreements, fees paid, territory boundaries and opening deadlines for each location
  2. Choose your lead location: compare Lisle, Chicago and Maywood on site readiness, build vs. conversion, and market strength
  3. Build your Unit 1 funding plan: lender pre-qualification, your equity contribution, and community or local incentive programs
  4. Hire your operator, then scale: bring on your general manager before opening, launch your training pipeline, and add locations two and three once the first is steady
