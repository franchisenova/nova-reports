FN Recommendation
Molly Maid — Proceed to Discovery Day  ·  Jan-Pro Regional as Second Conversation

Molly Maid is the right first move for Jordan. It is the only system on this analysis Jordan can pursue with current capital — the $109K low end fits today's liquid position without requiring the unconfirmed real estate equity. The recurring subscription model matches exactly what Jordan said on the intake form. Michigan territory is confirmed open. The 40-year system maturity and KKR-backed franchisor give it the most reliable Item 19 data and the most stable franchisor picture of the three. Jordan's PM background — scope management, team coordination, client communication — maps directly to running a team-leader-based Molly Maid operation.

Jan-Pro Regional is the second conversation, specifically the regional developer path. The regional structure is the most naturally semi-absentee model on this analysis and creates a passive income layer that Molly Maid and Meineke don't offer. The key questions to answer first: B2B sales comfort, Michigan regional territory availability, and exit market liquidity. Jordan's 2+ year horizon and portfolio-building instinct make this worth running in parallel. Unit-level entry today could serve as a low-cost proof of concept while Jordan builds toward regional developer status.

Meineke is not recommended at this stage. The 15% fee burden is a structural drag, the capital gap is significant without confirmed equity, and the revenue model is transactional — not the subscription structure Jordan explicitly asked for. Worth revisiting if real estate equity proves substantial and Jordan has deep automotive operational experience to manage the licensed service manager requirement.

Risks to Watch
  • Missing financial data must be collected before lender conversations begin. Real estate equity amount and outside investor commitment are both unconfirmed. Capital stack for Molly Maid is illustrative until these are known. Do not submit a lender package without them.
  • Credit building is the long pole. Jordan's 640–699 range puts SBA terms at a disadvantage. A focused 12–18 month plan could meaningfully change the picture — this is FN's primary near-term workstream for this candidate.
  • Semi-absentee SBA narrative. Any SBA lender will require documentation that Jordan has an identified team leader and that Molly Maid supports absentee operators. Both are true — this is about documentation and presentation, not substance.
  • Jan-Pro B2B sales motion. Jordan has no documented B2B sales background. The regional developer role requires active commercial business development. Assess comfort level explicitly before recommending Jan-Pro as a primary path.
  • Geography confirmation. This entire analysis assumes Michigan as primary market. If Jordan's actual priority is Research Triangle or Texas, territory analysis and FDD review need to be revisited for those markets.
