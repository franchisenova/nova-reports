This snapshot reflects where you stand today based on your intake responses — what you’re bringing to the table, where there’s room to grow, and what Franchise Nova can help you build. Your Readiness Profile
Operator Readiness & Capability

You bring more than 15 years of running things inside complex institutions — budgets, staffing, programs built from the ground up, and now the operations of a research center at Chicago Booth. That is exactly what a manager-led franchise needs from its owner: someone who hires well, reads the numbers, and holds people to a plan. Where you’ll grow is owning a full P&L, where payroll, sales and margin all land on you.

Vision, Motivation & Commitment

Your reason for franchising is unusually clear: a proven system paired with the operating and financial skills you already have, building equity for your family and good jobs in your community. Your 6–12 month timeline is realistic. The next step is turning “3 or more units” into a sequence — which territory first, and what has to be true before you open the second.

Sector & Franchise Fit

Senior Care and Technology are both built on customers who come back every week or month, and both can be run through a strong manager. Your multi-unit goal fits how strong home care owners grow — several territories run from one office. Keep your search inside these two sectors; restaurant models are built for full-time, hands-on operators, which isn’t the role you want.

Coachability & Ecosystem Fit

You’re asking the right questions before you buy — real owner earnings, staffing needs, and how involved the owner actually has to be. That mindset gets the most out of a franchise system. Your innovative streak is an asset when it’s aimed at hiring, execution and growth rather than redesigning a model that already works.

Strengths, Growth Areas & Opportunities

What You Bring

  • 15+ years of operations leadership across government, nonprofit and consulting — budgets, staffing and launches
  • An MBA in finance and strategy, plus experience overseeing an investment fund — you’ll read a franchise’s numbers critically
  • A cash-flow-first test that already accounts for a manager’s salary, fees and loan payments
  • Deep South Side roots and community ties — a real advantage for hiring caregivers and earning referrals

Things to Think Through

  • How hands-on you’ll be in year one — even manager-led businesses need the owner close while the manager ramps up
  • Your family financing: how much, and whether it comes in as a gift, an ownership stake or a loan — each shapes your loan options differently
  • Moving from budget oversight to full P&L ownership, including caregiver recruiting or IT sales
  • Building toward 680–700+ credit to widen your financing terms

What FN Can Unlock

  • A financing plan that turns your family backing into a lender-ready package
  • Connections to lending partners for SBA and community-lender options
  • Open South Side territories and existing businesses for sale that match your goals
  • A manager-hiring and reporting setup so a hands-off model actually works

Things to Stay Ahead Of

  • Most public payback estimates assume the owner works in the business — paying a manager adds time, so plan for it
  • Some systems you named require an involved owner or have limited territory in Chicago — confirm before you go deep
  • Getting your family financing in writing early keeps the timeline on track
Your Recommended Capital Pathway
Family Capital + SBA 7(a) Financing
Define the Family Investment · Pair It with SBA Lending · Grow Territory by Territory
Additional option: Buy an existing territory with seller financing, or use a community lender (CDFI)

Your liquid capital already covers the starting cost of a single home care territory and the minimum down payment on an SBA loan for a larger, multi-territory plan. Your uncle’s backing is the piece that decides how far and how fast you go — put in as a documented gift or ownership stake, it strengthens your down payment; as a loan, it adds a payment the business has to carry. Your credit range works with many SBA lenders, and moving it higher improves your terms.

Your path forward
  1. Discovery call: Settle your first-year involvement, which sector leads, and whether Chicago is the only market for unit one
  2. Family capital conversation: Agree on the amount and structure of your uncle’s support, and put it in writing
  3. Territory validation: Confirm open South Side territories, talk with owners who run through a general manager, and compare existing businesses for sale
  4. Financing & first signing: Package your lender application and sign territory one, with territories two and three sequenced behind it
